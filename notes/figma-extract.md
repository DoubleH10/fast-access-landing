## Typography
- **Primary typeface:** Inter Display (Medium 343×, Regular 61×, Bold 48×, SemiBold 15×, Light 1×)
- **Secondary typeface:** Geist (Regular 16×, Medium 6×, SemiBold 4×) — used for large display numerals (100–650px sizes)
- **Display alt:** Clash Grotesk (1×, 160px) — possibly used for one hero
- Letter spacing on display & body: `-0.030em` (tight)
- Line-height body: ~1.4, line-height display: ~1.2
- Body size: 24px / Caption: 20px / Heading: 48px / Display: 80px+
